2 Eligibility

dolcam is for people aged 13 or older. Children under 13 must not use it. If you are a minor, obtain your parent or guardian's consent to use the app and to make purchases.

3 App features

dolcam helps you take photos with pose guides, apply photo corrections, edit photos, and create framed images. Camera capture and ordinary photo processing take place on your device. Skin correction models also run on your device.

You can create pose outlines from photos in Faves. If you choose to make cleaner outlines, the app sends the photo to our relay server on Supabase. The relay uses OpenAI's image editing API and returns an outline. You can instead choose to create outlines only on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Respect the wishes and privacy of people you photograph. Follow the rules of the location and obtain any required consent or permission. Do not take photos in dangerous situations or disturb others.

Import only photos you have permission to use. Check the necessary copyright, image rights, and other permissions. Being able to view a photo does not by itself give you permission to use it. If you choose external processing, your permission must cover that processing too.

Use outlines created from imported photos as references for private photography. Converting a photo into an outline does not remove rights in the original photo. Do not sell or redistribute an outline or its source photo without the necessary rights holder's permission.

We do not acquire ownership of your photos. You permit us to process them only as needed to provide the features you use. If you choose external processing, this includes transmission and processing needed to create the outline. This permission does not authorize using your photos in advertising.

8 Storage and deletion

Photos and poses are stored on your device. Originals before correction may also be stored in the app. Exporting to the photo library is a separate action. The relay does not store photos. It stores outlines collected when the contribution setting is on. We may use outlines to create sample images featuring fictional people. See the Privacy Policy for details.

In the app’s Settings, go to Photo Import and choose Delete server account. After confirmation, the account and usage counter are deleted immediately. On success, the app removes its authentication session from the device and stops sending pending outlines. Photos, poses, and the contribution preference on your device are unchanged.

Collected outlines remain with their account ID link removed. Sample images already created from those outlines and catalog poses also remain, in a form not linked to an individual. Request records lose their account ID link and remain for the retention period below. Daily cost totals that are not linked to individuals also remain.

Deleting an account does not cancel a subscription. Apple will continue billing unless you cancel. Please cancel in Manage Subscriptions before continuing. You may also delete your account without canceling.

Processing records become eligible for deletion 90 days after completion. Records still being processed are not deleted. Anonymous accounts and usage counters become eligible for deletion 12 months after their last authenticated server use, including imports, allowance checks, and outline submissions. Automatic cleanup runs once daily and defers accounts with requests still being processed. You can delete your account in Settings even while processing is underway.

Collected outlines and samples created from them have no fixed retention end date. Once the account link is removed, we cannot identify your outlines or locate and delete them in response to a deletion request. Daily cost totals have no fixed retention end date because they are not linked to individuals. Uninstalling the app does not notify the server. The next import after account deletion creates a new anonymous account.

Deleting photos in the app does not remove copies in the photo library. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your server account or cancel subscriptions. Anonymous authentication credentials may remain in Keychain. OpenAI’s own API terms and retention policies govern its storage and use.
